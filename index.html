World Meeting Planner

How to use

Drag the slider to change the time in Vietnam. Every city changes too. Green means people are at work.

Say it

Seoul is 2 hours ahead of Vietnam.
London is 6 hours behind Vietnam.
Let's have a call at 9 a.m. your time.

Good to know

Some cities, like London, New York and Sydney, change their clocks in summer and winter. This page always uses today's real times.
